## 1. Target product's functionalities

StudyFlow is a desktop study planner that keeps a student's academic life in one place:

- **Personalized scheduling** — students add and manage their own courses, assignments, exams and deadlines.
- **Progress & workflow tracking** — academic life is organized into structured tasks whose daily/weekly completion is tracked.
- **Smart reminders & time management** — proactive deadline reminders reduce stress and prevent missed submissions.
- **Flexible task breakdown** — large assignments can be split into smaller sub-tasks, each with its own state.

## 2. Target users, stakeholders and role players

- **Primary users:** university and college students who juggle multiple courses and struggle with deadlines and time management.
- **Secondary users / stakeholders:** course instructors, project evaluators and academic mentors who benefit from organized student workflows.
- **Role players:** the *Student* (primary actor) and the *Reminder timer* (an internal background actor that watches approaching deadlines).

## 3. Problem solved and customer value

**The problem:** students feel overwhelmed by scattered assignments, overlapping exam dates and poor time management, which creates unnecessary stress and academic friction.

**The value:** StudyFlow centralizes every academic task into a single, intuitive application, giving students clear visibility and smart tracking so they can take control of their schedule, boost productivity and lower stress.
